Frequently asked questions

How do I steer?

By default, use the on-screen buttons. In Settings you can switch to tilt steering (and adjust its sensitivity) or turn on auto-accelerate. You can also play with a game controller or, on iPad, a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space to brake, Esc or P to pause.

Which game controllers work?

Any controller that works with iPhone or iPad, such as Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers. Use the stick or d-pad to steer, the triggers or face buttons for throttle and brake, and the menu button to pause.

How do I change the camera view?

Go to Settings and choose a camera: the smooth chase cam, a locked rear view, or first-person from the cockpit.

My lap times aren't showing on the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboards need Game Center. Check Settings > Game Center and make sure you're signed in.
  • Only lap times from Time Trial are posted, and only your personal best for each track and car class.
  • If you set a time while offline or signed out, it's posted the next time you open the game while signed in.
Can I play without Game Center?

Yes. Everything works without it. Your best times are saved on your device, but they won't appear on the global leaderboards.

What is the ghost car?

In Time Trial, a see-through ghost replays your best lap for that track and car class so you can race against yourself. You can turn it off in Settings.

Are the cars and tracks real?

No. Every car, team, driver and track in Retro Racer is fictional, inspired by the look of different racing eras.

How do I reset my progress?

Your records, ghosts, achievements and championship progress are saved on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the game resets them. Scores already posted to Game Center stay with your Game Center account.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, neither. See the privacy policy for how the game handles your data.
