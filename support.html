Frequently asked questions

How do I steer?
  • iPhone and iPad: use the on-screen buttons. In Settings you can switch to tilt steering (and adjust its sensitivity) or turn on auto-accelerate.
  • Mac, or iPad with a keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space to brake, Esc or P to pause. Return or Space skips the pre-race flyover.
  • Apple TV: use the Siri Remote or a game controller (see below).
  • Any device: pair a game controller.
Which game controllers work?

Any controller that works with your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV, such as Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers. Use the stick or d-pad to steer, the triggers or face buttons for throttle and brake, and the menu button to pause. A skips the pre-race flyover. On Mac and Apple TV, crashes and kerbs rumble the controller, if it supports rumble.

How do I play with the Siri Remote?

Hold the remote upright, like a TV remote. Slide your thumb left or right on the touch surface to steer, and click to brake. The throttle stays on by itself, because the remote has no spare button for it. Press Play/Pause or the Back (Menu) button to pause. For full control of the throttle, pair a game controller.

How do I change the camera view?

Go to Settings and choose a camera: the smooth chase cam, a locked rear view, or first-person from the cockpit.

My lap times aren't showing on the leaderboard.
  • Leaderboards need Game Center. Check Settings > Game Center and make sure you're signed in.
  • Only lap times from Time Trial are posted, and only your personal best for each track and car class.
  • If you set a time while offline or signed out, it's posted the next time you open the game while signed in.
Can I play without Game Center?

Yes. Everything works without it. Your best times are saved on your device, but they won't appear on the global leaderboards.

What is the ghost car?

In Time Trial, a see-through ghost replays your best lap for that track and car class so you can race against yourself. You can turn it off in Settings.

Are the cars and tracks real?

No. Every car, team, driver and track in Retro Racer is fictional, inspired by the look of different racing eras.

How do I reset my progress?

Your records, ghosts, achievements and championship progress are saved on each device. Deleting and reinstalling the game resets them. Scores already posted to Game Center stay with your Game Center account.

Does my progress carry over between my iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV?

Your Game Center leaderboard times and achievements follow your Game Center account to every device. Records, ghost laps, settings and championship progress are saved separately on each device.

On Apple TV, the system may clear ghost laps if it runs low on storage. Your records and settings are kept.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later (the game plays in landscape), Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and Apple TV with tvOS 17 or later.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No, neither. See the privacy policy for how the game handles your data.
