Privacy Policy

Retro Racer · Effective and last updated September 27, 2026

The short version
  • Retro Racer has no account of its own. The developer runs no server and receives none of your data.
  • If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best lap times and achievements are posted there, and other players can see your Game Center name and times on the leaderboards.
  • Everything else, including settings, records, ghost laps and championship progress, stays on your device.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who we are

Retro Racer is made by Omar Saraireh, an independent developer based in Canada ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email omorasaraira@gmail.com.

What Retro Racer uses and why

Game Center (optional)

Retro Racer uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. When the game starts, it checks whether you're signed in to Game Center. You don't have to be, and the game works fully without it.

If you are signed in:

  • Apple gives the game your Game Center display name and an anonymous player ID, so it can show your name and highlight your row on the leaderboards.
  • The game posts your best lap time for each track and car class to that track's global leaderboard. Lap times you set while signed out are posted the next time you're signed in.
  • The game reports your achievement progress to Game Center.
  • Other players can see your Game Center display name and lap times on the leaderboards, and the game shows you theirs.

This information is sent to and stored by Apple, not by us. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy; see also Apple's Game Center & Privacy page.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (controls, camera, sound, units and so on) and your chosen car colour.
  • Your best lap times and ghost laps for time trials.
  • Unlocked achievements and championship progress.

This is stored only on your device, and in your device backups if you use them. We never receive it.

Motion sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your device's motion sensors while you race to turn tilting into steering. This data is used instantly, on your device, and is never stored or sent anywhere. iOS doesn't ask permission for this kind of motion data.

Game controllers and keyboards

If you connect a game controller or keyboard, the game reads button presses only to control your car.

Third-party services

The only outside service Retro Racer uses is Apple Game Center, as described above. The game makes no other network connections.

What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in or profile of our own.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.
  • No chat and no way for players to message each other.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo access.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • On your device: your settings, records and progress stay until you delete Retro Racer, which removes them.
  • With us: nothing. We have no server and store no data about you.
  • In Game Center: Apple keeps your leaderboard scores and achievements with your Game Center account. To stop posting new scores, sign out of Game Center. You can manage or delete your Game Center data through your Apple Account at privacy.apple.com. If you'd like a specific score removed from a Retro Racer leaderboard, email us and we'll remove it where App Store Connect allows.

Settings you control

  • Game Center: Settings > Game Center. Sign out to play without posting scores. You can also change your Game Center nickname and profile privacy there.
  • Tilt steering: turn it off in the game's Settings screen by choosing button steering.
  • Parental controls: Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > Game Center lets parents limit Game Center features on a child's device.

Children's privacy

Retro Racer is a racing game suitable for all ages, and we know younger players may enjoy it. That's why it is built to collect nothing itself:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
  • There are no ads, no purchases, no chat and no way to contact other players.
  • The only information other players can see is a Game Center display name and lap times, and only if the player is signed in to Game Center. Apple manages Game Center accounts for children through Family Sharing, including a parent's consent and Screen Time controls.

If you're a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights over your personal information, including to access it, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. Because Retro Racer doesn't send us any of your data, we usually have nothing to access or delete, but we'll gladly help. Email omorasaraira@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

For Game Center data, you can also contact Apple through privacy.apple.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If Retro Racer's data practices change, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. For significant changes, we'll also mention it in the game's release notes on the App Store.
