Flat out through the 16-bit era. A pseudo-3D arcade racer with period formula and GT cars, pixel-perfect tracks and a chiptune soundtrack.

Coming soon_ iPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple TV

Requires iOS or iPadOS 17, macOS 14 Sonoma, or tvOS 17 or later. Free on all of them, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Game modes

Time Trial

Chase your best lap against your own ghost car, then post your time to Game Center leaderboards for every track and car class.

Race vs AI

Take on 5, 9 or 15 rivals over 3, 5 or 10 laps, on Easy, Medium or Hard. Start on pole, at the back, or somewhere random.

Championship

Build a season of up to five rounds and fight for the title across the points table.

Six eras of racing

Pick your car class

  • Formula '60s
  • Formula '70s
  • Formula '80s Turbo
  • Formula '90s
  • GT '90s
  • GT3 Modern

Every car, team and driver is fictional, and each class drives differently.

Paint it your way

Choose from 16 retro colours in the Garage.

Six fictional tracks

  • Harbourline Circuit
  • Redstone Ring
  • Valmonte Heights
  • Nordberg Night
  • Monteluce Street Circuit
  • Eschenwald Ring

Play your way

Controls

On-screen buttons or tilt steering on iPhone and iPad, a keyboard on Mac, the Siri Remote on Apple TV, and game controllers everywhere, with rumble on the controller. Auto-accelerate is there if you want it.

Three cameras

Smooth chase cam, locked rear view, or a first-person cockpit view.

Achievements

Ten Game Center achievements to unlock, from your first podium to a championship grand slam.

Play it everywhere

iPhone

Race anywhere with on-screen pedals or tilt steering, and feel every kerb and bump through the haptics.

iPad

The same pixel-perfect racing on a bigger screen. Plug in a keyboard or pair a controller if you want.

Mac

A resizable window and full keyboard controls: arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space to brake. Or pair a controller.

Apple TV

Big-screen racing from the sofa. Swipe the Siri Remote to steer and click to brake, or grab a controller for full throttle control.

No ads. No tracking.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no analytics. Only your lap times are shared, through Game Center, if you choose to sign in. Read the privacy policy.
